### Introduction to Volume I
- After stating why the report is submitted legally, the first line of the entire document is "The Russian government interfered in the 2016 presidential election in sweeping and systematic fashion." Despite how much others wish to contend about 'collusion,' this is the primary takeaway of the investigation.
