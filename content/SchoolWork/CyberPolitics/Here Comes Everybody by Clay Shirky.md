## Chapter 2 - SHARING ANCHORS COMMUNITY
- Starting off with using the example of the birthday paradox to explain the exponential growth in group connections make as they get bigger.
- General argument made for why organizations crop up, and why they perform well despite their high cost
- Several examples of how Flickr, a photo-sharing service, and blogging allowed for reports from individuals related to various events. Gives the overall argument that these organizations allowed individuals to have a lower cost in sharing and disseminating information and media.
- Gives an example of how modern organization charting(regional and local management) was a byproduct of attempting to manage large railroad sections.
- Goes over how drops in cost affects business operations and development, according to Coase theorem. Points out what happens when you have a large drop in costs
- Good overview of theories regarding organization, and how the introduction of technology lessens the cost of organizing, producing new structures of organizing that were generally theoretical before.
